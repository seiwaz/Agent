## A. Backtest integrity

### A1. Limit fill rule

Code: `lifecycle.advance` (PENDING branch): a long limit fills when `bar.low <= entry` (touch), a short when `bar.high >= entry`; the fill minute's stop counts, its target does not. Test: `tests/smc/test_diagnostics.py::test_fill_on_touch_versus_through`.

| population | market | filled (touch) | filled only by a bare touch | their R | filled (through) | total R touch → through |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| current | BTCUSDT | 2 | 0 | — | 2 | -2.00 → -2.00 |
| current | XRPUSDT | 1 | 0 | — | 1 | -1.00 → -1.00 |
| wide | BTCUSDT | 17 | 0 | — | 17 | -6.42 → -6.42 |
| wide | XRPUSDT | 18 | 0 | — | 18 | -14.34 → -14.34 |

A resting maker limit on Tabdeal fills only when a trade prints at the order's price and the queue in front of it is consumed; a bare touch (low == entry) does not guarantee a fill. Touch is the optimistic reading, through the conservative one.

### A3. Fees and R (worked examples)

Code: `lifecycle.risk_unit` (R unit = stop distance + entry fee + slippage + taker fee on the slipped stop fill), `Tracked.net_per_unit` (move − entry fee − exit fee), `wallet.part_pnl` (fees = qty × price × rate, i.e. on notional, once each; maker on a limit entry, taker on every exit). Leverage only sets the margin, never the fees. Test: `test_fees_are_charged_once_on_notional_maker_in_taker_out`.

- **BTCUSDT** (SHORT, SL, 2026-02-09): entry 70345.5, stop 70891.5, target 68335.8, exit 70914.610629; qty 0.00144 (1 % of 100 USDT over the R unit 692.7559, capped by 10× margin); notional 101.30 USDT; entry fee (maker) 0.0810, exit fee (taker) 0.0970, stop slippage inside the exit price 0.0333; gross -0.8195, net -0.9976 USDT; R = -1.000 (= net / (qty × R unit) = -1.000).
- **XRPUSDT** (SHORT, SL, 2026-04-05): entry 1.31087, stop 1.31515, target 1.27884, exit 1.3155787389; qty 142.7 (1 % of 100 USDT over the R unit 0.0070, capped by 10× margin); notional 187.06 USDT; entry fee (maker) 0.1496, exit fee (taker) 0.1783, stop slippage inside the exit price 0.0612; gross -0.6719, net -0.9999 USDT; R = -1.000 (= net / (qty × R unit) = -1.000).

Findings: fees are charged once, on notional; maker in, taker out; the stop pays the slippage allowance, the target pays none.
